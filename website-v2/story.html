Checked before we trusted it

We tested our own data first, and we say openly what the tool cannot know.

What we checked

Two NASA missions agreeWhen GPM sees rain, SMAP sees the soil less dry the next month. Correlation −0.81.
Nothing lost in the merge9 files became 60,480 rows. The few gaps in the FAO map are filled and flagged.
Trends are real, not luck560 trend tests, corrected so that random noise is not called a trend.
This page matches our analysisThe field you pick is scored live by our backend, with the same data and formula as our notebooks. All 556 farm squares give the same first choice as notebook 11.
We caught two data problemsFAO's model scores sugar beet as a summer crop, but here it grows in winter. And the elevation data shows slopes that are really trees and buildings. We did not let either one rank crops.

What it cannot know

  • Prices and markets"What farmers already grow" stands in for markets, mills and contracts.
  • Rice uses even more waterFAO's numbers leave out water lost from flooded paddies, so the real saving is bigger.
  • One square is many farmsThe answer is a starting point for a visit and a soil test, not an order for one plot.
